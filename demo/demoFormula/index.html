AERO / 01

DOWNFORCE

Wings and underfloor aerodynamics generate a force towards the track, increasing tyre loading and helping the car corner at higher speeds.

KEY PRINCIPLES

Bernoulli suction on inverted aerofoils and Newtonian downwash airflow deflection.

ENGINEERING TRADE-OFF

Greater downforce raises cornering grip but increases induced drag, penalizing straight-line top speed.

[ AERODYNAMIC DOWNFORCE & WAKE CONTROL ]

FRONT WING MAINPLANE & FLAPS

LOCATION:

At the leading edge of the vehicle, positioned forward of the front wheels and chassis nose.

FUNCTION:

Manages incoming undisturbed airflow, contributes 25% to 35% of overall vehicle downforce, and conditions aerodynamic wake around the front wheels.

ENGINEERING PRINCIPLE:

Multi-element inverted cambered aerofoils generating intense low pressure on the underside and high pressure above. Upper flap angles are adjustable to trim balance.

PERFORMANCE IMPACT:

Directly dictates front-axle grip, high-speed corner entry stability, turn-in authority, and overall vehicle aerodynamic balance.

ENGINEERING TRADE-OFF:

Increasing wing flap angle generates vital front downforce but induces parasitic profile drag and shifts the aerodynamic center of pressure forward as speed climbs.
